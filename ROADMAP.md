# KING AI Customer OS · Public Roadmap

## English

### Now
- improve approved public-knowledge answering;
- strengthen lead capture, feedback and human handoff;
- improve multilingual customer experiences;
- keep public/private knowledge boundaries explicit.

### Next
- richer customer journeys and product guidance;
- stronger analytics and relationship context;
- more reusable integrations across KING AI products;
- business-specific knowledge and service workflows.

### Longer term
- unified customer operating layer;
- governed omnichannel service and sales assistance;
- deeper CRM and partner integrations;
- enterprise customer intelligence with explicit privacy and authorization boundaries.

Roadmap items are directional until explicitly released and verified.

# 中文

### 当前重点
- 提升基于批准公开知识的回答质量；
- 强化线索获取、反馈和人工接管；
- 优化多语言客户体验；
- 始终明确公开知识与私有知识边界。

### 下一阶段
- 更完整的客户旅程和产品引导；
- 更强的分析和客户关系上下文；
- 在 KING AI 多个产品之间形成更可复用的集成；
- 支持企业专属知识和服务流程。

### 长期方向
- 统一客户操作层；
- 受治理的多渠道客服与销售辅助；
- 更深入的 CRM 与合作伙伴集成；
- 在明确隐私和授权边界下形成企业客户智慧。

路线图内容在正式发布和验证前均属于方向，不代表已经全面可用。
